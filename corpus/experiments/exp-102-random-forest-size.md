# Experiment 102: Random forest size (n_estimators)

## Setup
Dataset: scikit-learn digits (1797 samples, 64 features, 10 classes). Model: RandomForestClassifier with default depth, varying the number of trees. Evaluation: stratified 5-fold cross-validation,
shuffled, seed 1234. Scores are mean ± standard deviation across folds, in percentage points.
Run on 2026-09-21 with scikit-learn 1.9.1, Python 3.12.10.

## Results
| n_estimators | accuracy (%) | fit time per fold (s) |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 95.1 ± 1.2 | 0.13 |
| 50 | 97.3 ± 1.0 | 0.31 |
| 100 | 97.7 ± 0.7 | 0.37 |
| 300 | 97.9 ± 1.0 | 2.62 |

Fit time is the mean seconds to train one fold on this machine; compare it only within this report.

## Conclusion
Best accuracy: n_estimators=300 (97.9 ± 1.0). The gap to n_estimators=100 is 0.2 points, smaller than the fold-to-fold standard deviation (1.0), so the two are not clearly different. Fit time per fold went from 0.13 s with 10 trees to 2.62 s with 300 trees on this machine.
